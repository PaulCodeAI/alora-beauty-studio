Good to know

Before your visit.

Do I need a consultation?

For colour, major changes and corrective work, yes. It helps the stylist understand your hair history and set a realistic plan.

Can I request a specific stylist?

Yes. Add the stylist's name to your booking request and the team will confirm availability.

How early should I book?

For regular services, booking a few days ahead is recommended. Occasion and colour appointments are best planned earlier.

Do you take walk-ins?

Walk-ins are welcome when availability allows, but appointments receive priority.
